10 Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take? +
Up to 48 hours after we verify your payment, and usually far quicker. Delivery starts once you send your Order ID to @satishai21 on Telegram using the button shown after checkout.
Why is delivery manual and not instant? +
Every order is verified by hand before the file and key are issued. This keeps the product from being scraped or resold automatically, and means you always have a real person to talk to if something goes wrong.
What is the difference between the app, the key, and the bundle? +
The app is the ZYRA program itself. The activation key unlocks its full features on one device. The bundle is both together at a reduced price — that is what most people need if they are starting fresh.
Do I need my own Gemini API key? +
Yes. Get one free from aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and paste it into ZYRA on first run. It stays on your computer — we never see or store it. Any usage limits on that key are set by Google, not by us.
Can I use one key on two computers? +
No. Each key binds to the first device it activates on. If you have genuinely changed or reset your machine, message us with your Order ID and we will review it.
Does ZYRA record my screen or my voice? +
Nothing is sent to us. Audio and screen frames are processed on your computer and sent directly to Google's Gemini API using your own API key, only while you have the feature switched on.
What are the system requirements? +
Windows 10 or 11, a microphone, and an internet connection. Headphones are strongly recommended — they stop ZYRA from hearing its own voice through the mic, which makes conversations far smoother.
Do I get future updates? +
Yes. ZYRA has a built-in update check. When a new version is published you will be told, and you can download it from this site. There is no extra charge.
Is it a subscription? +
No. Every purchase is one-time. There is no recurring charge, no renewal and no auto-debit of any kind.
Can I get a refund? +
Before delivery, yes — full refund. After delivery, refunds are limited to the specific cases in section 4, because a digital file cannot be returned once it is in your hands.
Is my payment secure? +
Payments run through Razorpay. Your card, UPI and banking details go to them directly — they never pass through our website and we never store them.
Can I resell the software? +
No. The licence is personal and non-transferable. Reselling or redistributing it breaches the licence and will result in the key being deactivated.
